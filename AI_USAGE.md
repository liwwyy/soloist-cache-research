# AI involvement

This project was developed with an OpenAI Codex AI assistant. The repository owner supplied the research direction, authorized local experiments, provided a local Spotify Soloist credential, and paired the test device.

AI was used for source research, binary disassembly analysis, debugger probe design, writing and executing experimental Python/C code, interpreting results, and drafting this documentation. The published scripts and write-up are substantially AI-generated.

Claims are limited to the measurements actually observed in this session. There has been no independent replication or separate human security audit. Some initial hypotheses failed; those failures and the remaining limitations are documented rather than treated as established mechanisms.

AI-generated code and explanations can contain mistakes. A passing container checksum establishes consistency, not account safety, permission from Spotify, or compatibility with another binary. No claim of Spotify endorsement is made.
